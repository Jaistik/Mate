About MATE

Connecting people with skilled professionals.

What is MATE?

MATE — Hire a Professional is a platform designed to make it easier for users to discover and hire skilled professionals for the services they need. Professionals can create their profiles, showcase their skills and services, manage bookings, and keep track of their work.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple. Direct. Professional.

Find

Explore professionals by their services, categories, experience and location.

Book

Select a professional and send a booking request for the date and time you need.

Get It Done

Manage your booking, complete the service, and share your experience with a review.

FOR USERS

Find the right professional

Users can explore professional profiles, compare services, submit booking requests, receive booking updates and review completed services.

FOR PROFESSIONALS

Showcase your skills
